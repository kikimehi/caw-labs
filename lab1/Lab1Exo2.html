The Gaza genocide has resulted in devastating loss of life and infrastructure since October 7, 2023. As of October 2025, over 67,000 Palestinians have been killed in Gaza. About 30% of the dead are children. Entire neighborhoods have been destroyed by continuous bombardment.

According to UN Women, more than 28,000 women and girls have been killed since the war began. In casualty lists, of the ~60,199 known killed, 10,271 were women, 10,656 were boys, and 7,801 were girls.

Al Jazeera reports that at least 17,400 children have been killed , with many more uncounted. UNICEF states that over 50,000 children have been killed or injured.
