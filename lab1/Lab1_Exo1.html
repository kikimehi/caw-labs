Brief History

The history of Palestine spans thousands of years, marked by ancient civilizations and empires. It is also known as the birthplace of Prophet Jesus, making it a land of immense religious significance.

Over centuries, Palestine has witnessed major historical events, including the rise of great religions and the rule of empires such as the Romans and Ottomans.

Since 1948, Palestine has faced colonialism and ongoing conflict, becoming a symbol of resilience, struggle, and the pursuit of freedom.
